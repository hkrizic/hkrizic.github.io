Model strong gravitational lenses with ALPACA: lensed quasars and galaxy–galaxy lenses, a pixelated source, a PSF fitted together with the lens, and time-delay cosmography. Learn it on real data, look up exactly what every option does, or open a finished run and explore it.

Tutorial
Two complete walkthroughs on real telescope data: the lensed quasar HE0435−1223 (JWST) and the galaxy–galaxy lens COSMOS 5921+0638 (HST). From the raw image to a model you can judge, with every figure explained.
