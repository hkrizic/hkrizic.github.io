Frequently asked

  • Where is my data? Only on your iPhone. Settings → Data shows the automatic backups and lets you export, restore or reset.
  • Does it need Apple Health? No, but with it your steps, scale weigh-ins, runs and swims count automatically.
  • Is the AI required? No. Turn it on in Settings → AI: Apple Intelligence runs on your iPhone, ChatGPT is optional and uses your own account.
  • Siri? Say “Hey Siri, I ate healthy in FitDojo”, “… rest day in FitDojo” or “… what's left today in FitDojo”.
