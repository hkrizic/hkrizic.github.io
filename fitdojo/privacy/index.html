Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026, updated for version 1.3

FitDojo is a fitness and healthy-eating app for iPhone. It is built so that your data stays on your iPhone. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • FitDojo has no account and no server. Everything you log is stored only on your iPhone.
  • There is no tracking, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDK in the app.
  • Apple Health data is used only to run the app's features and is never sold, shared for advertising or used for anything else.
  • AI features are optional. Apple Intelligence runs on your iPhone. ChatGPT is used only if you connect your own ChatGPT or OpenAI account, and then only the information described below is sent to OpenAI.

Who is responsible

Hrvoje Krizic, Geneva, Switzerland ("we"). Contact: through the contact section at hrvojekrizic.ch. We process personal data in line with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where it applies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

What FitDojo stores on your iPhone

  • Your profile: the name you enter, start weight, goal weight and height, and your training answers (goal, fitness level, equipment, workouts per week, session length).
  • Your daily logs: whether you ate healthy or had a cheat day, your chosen activity, rest days, workouts and their results (weights used, how they felt), walks, runs and swims, weigh-ins, streak, XP and badges.
  • If you switch food logging to calories: the meals you log (name, calories, protein, carbs, fat), your daily targets, and, only if you enter them for the suggested target, your sex and year of birth.
  • Your settings, your units, your dumbbell equipment, your week plan, your favourite and saved recipes.
  • Automatic backups of the above (one per day for 14 days, and before updates or resets).

This data is kept in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot see it. It may be included in your own iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) if you have device backups turned on; those backups are controlled by you and Apple. You can delete everything at any time (see "Your choices").

Progress photos

Progress photos are optional. You take them with the camera inside FitDojo. They are saved only inside the app, with the iPhone's strongest file protection, and never in your photo library, never uploaded and never sent to any AI. Viewing them requires Face ID (or your passcode), and the gallery locks again as soon as you leave the app. They are not part of FitDojo's own backups or data export; like all app data they may be included in your own encrypted iPhone backup. You can delete any photo in the gallery, and deleting the app deletes all of them.

Camera access is used only to take these photos. Face ID is handled by iOS; FitDojo never sees your face data.

Apple Health

If you allow it, FitDojo reads from Apple Health: step count, body weight, workouts (runs, swims and walks recorded by other apps or your Apple Watch) and their distance. FitDojo writes to Apple Health: weigh-ins you enter and workouts you finish in the app.

Health data is used only to show your progress, tick off walks, runs and swims, count weigh-ins from a smart scale, and save your workouts. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with anyone — with one exception you control: if you connect ChatGPT (below), a short summary of your recent activity can be part of the AI coach's request. You can change Health permissions any time in the Health app or in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → FitDojo.

Notifications and Siri

Reminders are local notifications created on your iPhone; nothing is sent to a server. Siri and Shortcuts commands (for example "I ate healthy in FitDojo") are handled by Apple's Siri according to Apple's privacy policy; FitDojo only receives the action and stores the result on your iPhone.

AI features (optional)

You choose the AI in Settings → AI (Automatic, Apple Intelligence, ChatGPT or Off). With AI off, no AI is used.

Apple Intelligence (on-device). FitDojo uses Apple's on-device language model to suggest today's activity, write your weekly recap and turn ingredients you type into a recipe. This runs on your iPhone; FitDojo does not send this data anywhere.

ChatGPT (only if you connect it). If you choose "Continue with ChatGPT" (your ChatGPT plan) or enter your own OpenAI API key, FitDojo sends requests directly from your iPhone to OpenAI. Depending on the feature, a request contains:

  • for the daily suggestion and weekly recap: a short text summary of the last two weeks — which workouts, walks, runs, swims and rest days you logged, whether you logged healthy or cheat days, daily step counts, your weight trend and goal, your streak and remaining rest/cheat days, and your training answers (goal, fitness level, equipment, workouts per week, session length);
  • for recipes: the ingredients you typed.

It does not contain your name, your meals or your progress photos. Requests are sent under your OpenAI account and are governed by OpenAI's terms and privacy policy (openai.com/policies). FitDojo asks OpenAI not to store the response (store: false); OpenAI may still keep data as described in its own policies. Sign-in tokens or your API key are stored in your iPhone's Keychain and never leave your device except to talk to OpenAI. You can disconnect at any time in Settings → AI → ChatGPT.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no servers, no cloud database run by us.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or renting of any data.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data: Settings → Data → Reset progress (a backup is kept on the device so you can undo it), or delete the app, which removes all its data and backups from your iPhone.
  • Export your data: Settings → Data → Export backup file.
  • Apple Health: change or revoke access in the Health app.
  • AI: turn it off or disconnect ChatGPT in Settings → AI.
  • Under the FADP and GDPR you have the right to information, correction, deletion and to object. Because we do not receive your data, most requests are fulfilled by the controls above; you can also contact us.

Children

FitDojo is not directed at children under 13 and is meant for adults who want to train and lose weight.
